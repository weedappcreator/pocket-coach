Pocket Coach

Decision support for short-expiry binary options. It reads candles, runs seven named setups, prices the edge against the payout's break-even, and applies the trader's risk rules. It does not place trades, and it is built to refuse far more often than it approves.

Read this first. The backtest on real data currently shows a 42.3% win rate against a 52.1% break-even — the setup logic beats a coin flip by about 3 points but loses badly at a 92% payout. That is the honest result, and a system that hid it would be worthless. OTC candles here are a public-market proxy, not PocketOption's synthetic OTC feed.

What this system will not do

It will not place a trade. There is no order endpoint, by design.
It will not call a coin flip an edge. At a 92% payout, break-even is 52.08%. A 50% win rate loses 4% of every stake.
It will not treat public EUR/USD candles as PocketOption OTC. Those are different price processes; the proxy is labelled everywhere it appears.
It will not report a win rate without its confidence interval and its break-even. A win rate on its own is not evidence.
Pattern detection runs on numpy buffers (~26× faster than row-wise pandas). Signals are cached for 60s server-side. Nothing here is financial advice.
